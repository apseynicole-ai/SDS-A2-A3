# SDS188 A2S2 Announcement 1

**Posted by:** Zoe-Mae Adams  
**Date:** Monday, 21 September 2026, 11:36 AM

Good day students

The **A2S2** will take place on **9 November at 09:00**.

If you are concerned about possible clashes, please consult the assessment timetable to confirm whether a clash exists.

Please note that this assessment is organised by the Assessment Office. Any concession-related queries, including sport commitments or administrative issues, should therefore be sent directly to **stbexams@sun.ac.za**.

The A2S2 will be a **written, closed-book assessment**. The scope will include the full semester’s work, namely **Chapters 7, 8, 9, 10, 13 and 14**.

The assessment will be **three hours long** and will consist of two sections:

**Section A:** Longer questions where calculations must be shown.  
**Section B:** Multiple-choice questions.
