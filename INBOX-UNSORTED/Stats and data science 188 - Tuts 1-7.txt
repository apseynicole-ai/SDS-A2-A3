Card 1 — Recognising a Uniform Distribution
Question type
Identify whether the distribution is uniform.
Teach it simply
Uniform means:
“All values between the lower limit and upper limit have the same chance.”
So unlike the normal distribution, where values near the mean are more likely, the uniform distribution treats every value in the interval equally.
Think of it like a flat rectangle:
Position
	Probability density
	Near the start
	Same height
	In the middle
	Same height
	Near the end
	Same height
	The Chapter 6 transcript describes the uniform distribution as a rectangular distribution with a starting value a and an ending value b.

Card 4 — Why Continuity Correction Is Needed

Teach it simply

0,1,2,3,…,n0,1,2,3,\dots,n0,1,2,3,…,n

The transcript describes this as the “tax” paid when shifting from discrete to continuous.
