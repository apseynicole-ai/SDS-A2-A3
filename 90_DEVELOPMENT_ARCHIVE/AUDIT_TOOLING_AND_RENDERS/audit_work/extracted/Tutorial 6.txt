4. ‘n Stel finale kursus punte in ‘n inleidende statistiek kursus is normaal verdeel, met ‘n 
gemiddelde van 73 (uit 100) en ‘n standaardafwyking van 8. /  
A set of final course grades in an introductory statistics course is normally distributed, 
with a mean of 73 (out of 100) and a standard deviation of 8. 
a. Wat is die waarskynlikheid dat ‘n student minder as 91 behaal het in die finale 
punt? /  
What is the probability that a student earned below 91 as the final grade? 
b. Wat is die waarskynlikheid dat ‘n student ‘n finale punt tussen 65 en 89 behaal 
het? / 
What is the probability that a student earned a final grade between 65 and 89? 
c. Die waarskynlikheid is 5 persent dat ‘n student wat die kursus volg, hoër verdien 
as watter punt? /  
The probability is 5 percent that a student that takes the course earns higher 
than what grade? 
d. Die universiteit gebruik ‘n kurwe-graderingstelsel, sodanig dat tien persent van 
die studente wat vir ‘n kursus geregistreer ‘n onderskeiding kry , ongeag die 
punt. Is jy beter af as jy ‘n finale punt van 81 kry vir hierdie kursus, of as jy ‘n 
finale punt van 68 kry vir ‘n aparte kursus met ‘n gemiddelde van 62 en ‘n 
standaardafwyking van 3? Verduidelik jou antwoord? / 
The university operates a curve grading system such that the top ten percent of 
the students registered for a course gets a distinction, regardless of the score. 
Are you better off earning a final grade of 81 for this course or earning a final 
grade of 68 for a separate course that has a mean of 62 and a standard 
deviation of 3? Explain your answer?
